## 🔒 Safe Testing Policy

During college mini-project demonstrations and automated testing:

1. **RFC 2606 Reserved Domains**:
   - `example.com`
   - `example.org`
   - `example.net`
   - `.test`, `.invalid`, `.localhost`
2. **Synthetic Phishing Targets (Mock Data)**:
   - `http://192.168.1.1/paypal/verify.php`
   - `http://login.secure-account-verification-alert.com`
   - `http://wellsfargo.security-check.xyz`
3. **Strict Prohibition**:
   - Under no circumstances should team members fetch, download, or execute active malware or weaponized exploits.
